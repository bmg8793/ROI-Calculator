How the math works. "Projected difference" is the literal year-1 salary change between your current and target lanes. "Lifetime earnings gain" models a realistic teacher pay trajectory: linear progression from Starting Master's to Top of Master's over ~12 years (typical district step schedule), then steady at Top of Master's for any remaining years. The cumulative MA-lane earnings are compared to staying at your current Bachelor's-lane salary. Your district pay scale and time-to-top will vary — treat these as a directional guide, not a guarantee.

Source: National Education Association (NEA), Teacher Salary Benchmark Report 2025. Salary lane requirements vary by district.
